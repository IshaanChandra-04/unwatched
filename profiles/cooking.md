# Cooking

Keep: recipes, techniques, ingredient swaps, meal prep, restaurant dishes worth recreating.
Skip: restaurant reviews with no dish detail, diet debates, product ads without a recipe.
Extract per post: dish name, ingredients with quantities when spoken, the steps in order, timings and temperatures, the one tip the creator stresses, and the source.
Output: a recipe book grouped by meal type, a techniques section, a shopping list of recurring ingredients, then the transcripts of kept posts.
